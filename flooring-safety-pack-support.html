Flooring Safety Pack Support

Flooring Safety Pack is an Android app for commercial flooring safety documents, hot works records, contractor handoff and GPS sign-on/sign-off logs.

For help, email nick@commercialconcretegrinding.co.nz or call 0278358198.

01 Install issue

Check that the APK came from this website, then allow browser installs if Android asks.

02 GPS issue

Location is optional for sign-on/sign-off. Denying GPS keeps the app usable but records remain draft.

03 Data issue

Use Clear local data in the app to remove local project, worker and GPS records from the device.
