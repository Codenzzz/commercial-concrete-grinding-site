Concrete Grinding Services

Fast, practical floor preparation for commercial projects.

Commercial Concrete Grinding helps Auckland businesses get concrete floors ready for the next trade, coating, tenancy handover or flooring install. The work is direct: assess the slab, remove the problem surface, grind to the required finish and leave the floor ready for what comes next.

For commercial vinyl and Marmoleum projects, the floor preparation and finished installation can be planned together so the finished floor has a better chance of performing properly.

01 Warehouse and tenancy grinding

Grinding for warehouses, workshops, retail spaces, office fit-outs and commercial handovers.

02 Adhesive, paint and coating removal

Removal of old floor coatings, glue residue and surface contamination before new flooring work.

03 Flooring-ready preparation

Surface preparation for vinyl, Marmoleum, levelling compounds, coatings and resurfacing work.
